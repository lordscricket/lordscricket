About Us

Welcome to Lords Cricket Club – the home of Green Ball Cricket.

Green Ball Cricket is for those who want to play fun, friendly games without worrying about safety equipment, umpires and club commitments.

Yes you can have a cricket ‘career’ – with all your averages and stats and scoresheets, player ratings, match reports and – oh yes – the joy of playing cricket together.

Green Ball Cricket works just fine with players of mixed ages and abilities – and only 10 players are needed for a great game. There’s one simple page of rules – check it out here.

Keep an eye out here for up-coming games – or browse the stats and reports of our long history.

And stay posted for the capability to host your own games – we’re working on that.
